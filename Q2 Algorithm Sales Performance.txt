3. Declare a variable companyTotal and initialize it to 0.
4. Declare variables highestSales and topRep to keep track of the highest total sales and the representative who achieved it.
5. Use an outer for loop to go through each sales representative (row).
6. Initialize rowTotal to 0 at the beginning of each row.
7. Use an inner for loop to go through each week's sales (column).

10. Add rowTotal to companyTotal.
11. Compare rowTotal with highestSales. If rowTotal is greater, update highestSales and topRep.
12. Repeat for all representatives.

14. Display the representative with the highest total sales.
